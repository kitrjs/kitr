A kit's `kitr` block can carry a `kit` object: who the kit is for, three to five highlights, the preset's base kit, where its example output lives, the file to read first and the commands to run. `kitr package:check` validates it and `KitrKitInfo` types it. Every official kit now describes itself this way, and its homepage is its page on kitr.dev/kits.
